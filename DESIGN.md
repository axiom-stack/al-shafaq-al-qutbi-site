ALFS — Al Shafaq Al Qutbi Logistics Services
Design System & Brand Guidelines v1.0

1. Brand Identity
Company Name: Al Shafaq Al Qutbi Logistics Services (ALFS)
Brand Personality: Trustworthy, Dynamic, Regional Expert. Professional Levant-and-Gulf operator.
Positioning: Your Trusted Logistics Partner — From Jordan to the World

2. Color Palette
Primary Colors:
- ALFS Navy: #1A2F7A
- ALFS Royal Blue: #1E3DA8
- ALFS Deep Blue: #0D1F5C
Accent Colors:
- ALFS Orange: #F47920
- ALFS Amber: #E8650A
Neutral Colors:
- White: #FFFFFF
- Off-White: #F5F5F5
- Light Grey: #E0E0E0
- Warm Grey: #999999
- Dark Overlay: rgba(13, 31, 92, 0.75)

Rules:
- Orange is for accents/CTAs only, never large backgrounds.
- Blue is dominant.
- Section titles require a centered orange underline bar (3px height, ~50px width).
- Gradients: deep-blue-to-royal-blue.

3. Typography (English)
Typefaces: Montserrat (700 Bold for Logo/Brand Mark, 400-600 for Technical/Contact/Body)
Scale:
- display: 60-72px Bold
- h1: 40-48px Bold
- h2: 32-38px Bold
- h3: 24-28px Bold
- h4: 20-22px SemiBold
- body-lg: 18-20px Regular
- body: 16-17px Regular
Rules:
- Headings on dark blue: White
- Headings on light: ALFS Navy (#1A2F7A)
- Body line height: 1.6 - 1.7

4. UI Components
Buttons:
- Primary: #F47920 bg, white text, 8px radius, Cairo 600 (or Montserrat 600 for EN).
- Secondary: Transparent, 2px white border.
- Ghost: 2px ALFS Navy border.
Cards:
- Background: #1E3DA8 or #FFFFFF.
- Radius: 16-20px.
- Shadow: 0 4px 24px rgba(26, 47, 122, 0.12).
List Items:
- Orange circle-checkmark pattern.

5. Layout & Spacing
Grid: 12-column, 1280px max-width.
Spacing: 8px base (4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128).
Section Padding: 96px vertical (Desktop).
