ALFS Email Signatures

Three signature options aligned with the site design system (navy #1A2F7A, orange #F47920, surface #FBF8FF, Montserrat/Arial typography, 12px card radius, brand shadows). Copy the HTML inside each frame into your email client. Replace LOGO_URL with your hosted logo URL (e.g. https://www.aurora-lfs.com/images/ALFS_LOGO.png) before sending.

Design 1

Clean corporate horizontal

Mirrors the site’s horizontal header/footer rhythm: logo and identity on one row, contact details on a second. Navy typography, orange accent rule, and a soft divider echo card borders (#E8E4EF) used across pages. Best for everyday B2B correspondence where clarity and compact width matter.
